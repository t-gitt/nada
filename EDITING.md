# Editing your site

Everything is done at **[app.pagescms.org](https://app.pagescms.org)**. Sign in with GitHub and open
the portfolio. Changes go live about a minute after you press **Save**.

## Add a new artwork

1. In the left menu, open the room it belongs to, like **Room II · Charcoal**.
2. Scroll to **Artworks** and click **Add an entry**.
3. Click **Image** and upload the photo or scan. Any size is fine, straight from your phone.
4. If you like, fill in a **Title**, **Year**, and **Medium & size**. These show under the
   picture and in the full-screen view. Leave them empty and it shows as "Charcoal · 13".
5. Press **Save**.

## Reorder or remove artworks

In the room, drag artworks up or down by their handle, or use the item's menu to delete one. The
**first** artwork is the preview that appears when someone hovers over the room's name on the home page,
and the first one in the room's sideways row.

## Change how a room's artworks are framed

Each room has two frame settings:

- **Frame style in the row:** the sideways row on the main page.
- **Frame style inside the room:** the full page that opens from the door.

Each one can be:

- **Pinned paper:** taped to the wall and slightly tilted.
- **Sketched frame:** a rough, hand-drawn border.
- **Gallery frame:** a light mat inside a dark frame.
- **No frame:** just the artwork.

## Change any text

Open **Site text & photos**. Each part of the page has its own section: Welcome screen, The story,
Rooms intro, About (including the timeline), and Contact. To make a word *italic*, wrap it in
asterisks: `*Leave It Unspoken*`.

- **New exhibition or award:** About → Timeline → add an entry at the bottom.
- **Show an email address:** Contact → Email. Leave it empty to hide it.
- **New social link:** Contact → Social links.

## Change the photos of you

In **Site text & photos**: *The story → Small round photo* and *About → Portrait*.

## If something looks wrong

Every save is kept in history, so nothing is ever lost. Message whoever set up the site and they can
undo any change in seconds.
